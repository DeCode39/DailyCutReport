One honest daily picture

Less logging. Better decisions.

Daily Cut Report turns the numbers you already generate into a practical plan for the rest of your day.

01 · Balance

Your burn, intake and goal—together.

Health Connect activity meets your local food log to calculate a real daily energy balance, with history and weight trends in context.

MONTODAYSAT
02 · Capture

Scan it. Check it. Log it.

Use barcodes or multilingual nutrition-label OCR. Recognition happens on your device, and you approve every value.

03 · Create

Build a product from scratch.

Choose servings, grams, or milliliters when the label supports them, then enter nutrition, price, and planner behavior. Convertible products keep both quantities synchronized.

04 · Photo to JSON

Log from photos, all at once.

Photograph the product or nutrition label, ask your preferred external AI to estimate the values, then paste the result using Daily Cut Report’s JSON format. Review the filled draft before saving.

Privacy is the architecture

Your data doesn’t need an audience.

No internet permission. No account. No telemetry. Your food, photos and health records stay on the device you chose to trust.

Zero network accessThe build is verified to exclude internet and network-state permissions.
On-device recognitionBarcode recognition and English, Chinese and Japanese OCR run locally. No generative AI is built in.
Encrypted portabilityPassword-protected backups let you move or restore your data on your terms.
